# Question Bank

## Business
- What problem are we solving?
- Who is the primary user?
- What painful workflow are we replacing?
- What is the success metric?
- What is explicitly out of scope?

## Product
- What are the main user journeys?
- What are the must-have features?
- What are nice-to-have features?
- What are the failure states?
- What should the user never experience?

## Data
- What entities exist?
- What data is sensitive?
- What data must be auditable?
- What data can be deleted?
- What data needs retention rules?

## Users and permissions
- What user roles exist?
- What can each role do?
- What must each role never access?

## Technical
- Preferred stack?
- Deployment target?
- Authentication method?
- Payment system?
- External integrations?
- Performance requirements?

## UI/UX
- Main pages?
- Main flows?
- Empty states?
- Error states?
- Mobile requirements?
- Accessibility requirements?

## Testing
- What flows must never break?
- What needs E2E coverage?
- What edge cases are high-risk?
