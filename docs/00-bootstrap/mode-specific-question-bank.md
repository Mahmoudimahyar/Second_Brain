# Mode-Specific Question Bank

Use only the section that matches the selected project mode.

## Full product/business
- Who buys?
- Who uses?
- What is the conversion goal?
- What is the MVP?
- What pricing or business model matters?
- What marketing/sales docs should we include or skip?

## Internal tool
- Who inside the team uses this?
- What workflow is being automated?
- What input data does it need?
- What output/action should it produce?
- What permissions or sensitive data exist?
- What does success look like operationally?

## Developer tool
- Who is the developer user?
- What command/API will they use?
- What is the install/setup flow?
- What examples are required?
- What must the tool never break?
- How should versioning work?

## CLI tool
- What commands are needed?
- What are inputs/outputs?
- What config/env vars are needed?
- What errors should be shown?
- What exit codes matter?
- What examples should be documented?

## API service
- Who/what consumes the API?
- What endpoints are needed?
- What auth is required?
- What data model is needed?
- What rate limits/errors matter?
- What contract tests are needed?

## Library/package
- What is the public API?
- What languages/runtimes are supported?
- What examples are required?
- What compatibility guarantees exist?
- How should releases/versioning work?

## Research prototype
- What is the research question?
- What hypotheses are being tested?
- What data/simulations are needed?
- What metrics define success?
- How will results be reproduced?
- What limitations should be documented?

## Automation script
- What workflow is automated?
- What triggers the script?
- What inputs and outputs exist?
- What failure modes matter?
- What logs/reports are needed?
- How will it be run?

## Data pipeline
- What sources are ingested?
- What transformations happen?
- What schemas are required?
- What validation rules exist?
- What lineage/audit needs exist?
- What happens on failure?

## AI agent system
- What agents exist?
- What tools can they call?
- What memory/retrieval is needed?
- What evals prove it works?
- What safety boundaries exist?
- What token budget applies?

## Landing page only
- Who is the visitor?
- What is the desired action?
- What proof is available?
- What objections must be handled?
- What sections are needed?
- What analytics should be tracked?
