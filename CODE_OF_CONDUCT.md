# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**
as its code of conduct. The full text at that link applies to every project space —
issues, pull requests, discussions, and review threads.

## In short

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Critique code, designs, and evidence — not people.
- Disagreement is welcome; this project runs on adversarial review of its own claims
  (see `docs/00-bootstrap/why-drift-happened.md`). Harassment, personal attacks, and
  discriminatory language are not.
- No publishing of anyone's private information. That includes forum users: do not paste
  real usernames or identifiable post text into issues, PRs, fixtures, or docs.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact options on
the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Mahmoudimahyar), or — for anything
security- or privacy-related — through the private channel described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer follows the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines
(correction → warning → temporary ban → permanent ban), applied in proportion to the
impact of the behaviour.
